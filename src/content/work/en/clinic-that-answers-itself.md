## Problem

The front desk spent around three hours a day confirming appointments and answering the same questions on WhatsApp. Reminders depended on someone remembering to send them.

## System

A workflow that handles bookings, reminders and common questions on WhatsApp, and hands the conversation to a person when the question is medical. Every booking lands in the clinic's calendar without retyping.

## Result

The front desk got its three hours back each day. The system was live three weeks after signature.
