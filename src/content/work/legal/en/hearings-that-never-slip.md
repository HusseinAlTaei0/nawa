## Problem

Every hearing date lived in one diary kept by the office manager. Appeal periods were counted by hand, documents arrived on several phones, and twice in one quarter a date came too close for comfort.

## System

One record per matter with its court, parties, documents and dates. A shared calendar fed from those records, with alerts days before every hearing and time limit. Documents sent on WhatsApp are saved straight into the right matter. A lawyer confirms every time limit when it is entered.

## Result

No hearing was missed in the first six months, and the team stopped spending around nine hours a week looking for files. The system was live four weeks after signature.
