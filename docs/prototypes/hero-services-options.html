How to answer

Reply in the chat with one choice for each, for example Hero A, Services A. Mixing is fine: say which part you want from which option, such as the scene from Hero A with the larger headline from Hero B.

What these sketches leave out

  • Arabic headings use a stand-in font here. The site will use Najwa Arabic.
  • Buttons and links inside the sketches are not wired.
  • On the site, the orbit and the cards follow page scroll. Here they use arrows, clicks and swipes.
  • The language switch plays the eclipse transition, drawn as a dark disc with a gold rim.
  • Weak phones and reduced-motion settings get a still image in place of each moving canvas.
